## 📅 Day 3: Token Economics - Understanding AI's Currency

**Tweet:**

💰 **Day 3 of my AI Engineering Journey!** 💰

Today I went under the hood of LLMs and studied TOKENS — the currency of AI!

🔑 **What I learned:**
✅ What tokens are and why they matter
✅ Difference between prompt tokens, completion tokens & total tokens
✅ Using `max_tokens` to control response length
✅ Understanding `finish_reason` (why the model stopped)
✅ Processing multiple prompts in a loop and comparing token usage

**Sample Insights:**
- Short prompts = low token consumption
- Complex responses = higher completion tokens
- Token management is CRITICAL for cost optimization at scale
